Charging a Tesla in Texas
$44 a month at 15.91¢ per kWh

$44 a month. That's what 1,000 miles in a Model Y Long Range AWD costs on Texas's average home electricity price of 15.91¢ per kWh (August 2026, EIA). That's 12% below the US average of 18.16¢, and 22nd cheapest of 51 (50 states plus DC). Prices are up 3.3% from 15.40¢ in August 2025, so the same driving costs $1.40 more a month than a year ago.

Before you trust the average: 15.91¢ is what Texas homes paid on average, fees included. Your bill shows your real rate; divide the total by the kWh used. Supercharging usually costs more than home power, so road trips will run above these numbers.
