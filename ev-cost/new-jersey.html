Charging a Tesla in New Jersey
$68 a month at 24.61¢ per kWh

$68 a month. That's what 1,000 miles in a Model Y Long Range AWD costs on New Jersey's average home electricity price of 24.61¢ per kWh (August 2026, EIA). That's 36% above the US average and the 10th most expensive of 51 (50 states plus DC), so charging at home costs more here than almost anywhere. It barely moved from August 2025 (24.70¢).

Before you trust the average: 24.61¢ is what New Jersey homes paid on average, fees included. Your bill shows your real rate; divide the total by the kWh used. Supercharging usually costs more than home power, so road trips will run above these numbers.
