Charging a Tesla in Nebraska
$37 a month at 13.54¢ per kWh

$37 a month. That's what 1,000 miles in a Model Y Long Range AWD costs on Nebraska's average home electricity price of 13.54¢ per kWh (August 2026, EIA). That's 25% below the US average and the 7th cheapest of 51 (50 states plus DC), so Nebraska is one of the cheapest places in the country to charge at home. Prices are up 5.8% from 12.80¢ in August 2025, so the same driving costs $2.03 more a month than a year ago.
