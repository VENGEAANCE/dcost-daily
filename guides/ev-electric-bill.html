What an EV adds
to your power bill
about 275 kWh a month for a Model Y

275 kWh a month. That's what 1,000 miles in a Model Y Long Range AWD adds to your meter, using the EPA's 27.5 kWh per 100 miles. The EPA number is measured at the wall, so charging losses are already in it. At the US average of 18.16¢ that's $50 more on your bill.

Spotting it on the bill. Most homes use several hundred kWh a month before an EV, so a Model Y can be a large share of the jump in your first bill. If your utility has tiered pricing, those extra kWh can land in a pricier tier, so the real cost per kWh can sit above your state's average. If it offers a time-of-use or EV plan, charging overnight is where the savings are.
