How these are worked out. Power is amps times 240V (120V for the outlet), times about 90% for charging losses, divided by the car's EPA energy use per mile. Real numbers wobble with temperature and battery level.

The car sets the ceiling. A 48A charger only helps if the car's onboard charger takes 48A. Some trims accept less, so check your car's spec before paying for the bigger install.

When 120V is enough. If you drive under about 30 miles a day and park for 10+ hours, a plain outlet keeps up for a Model 3 or Model Y. A Cybertruck needs 1.6x the energy, so it struggles on 120V.
