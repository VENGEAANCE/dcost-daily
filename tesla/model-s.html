At the US average it costs $4.93 per 100 miles to charge, against $14.23 in gas for a 30 MPG SUV and $8.37 for a 51 MPG Camry Hybrid at $4.27 a gallon. A Model 3 Standard RWD uses 24.3 kWh, so it costs $5 less a month. A Model Y Long Range AWD uses 27.5 kWh, so it costs $1 more a month.

Charging speed at home: a regular 120V outlet adds about 5 miles per hour. 32A Level 2 adds about 25; 40A Level 2 adds about 32; 48A Level 2 adds about 38 miles per hour, if the car's onboard charger accepts that much. How many hours a night that means.
