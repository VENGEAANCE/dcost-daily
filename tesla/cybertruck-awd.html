$78 a month at the US average

$78 a month at the US average of 18.16¢ per kWh. The EPA rates the 2026 Cybertruck AWD at 42.9 kWh per 100 miles and 325 miles of range (fueleconomy.gov). Across the states that swings from $55 in Nevada to $202 in Hawaii.

At the US average it costs $7.79 per 100 miles to charge, against $14.23 in gas for a 30 MPG SUV and $8.37 for a 51 MPG Camry Hybrid at $4.27 a gallon. A Model 3 Standard RWD uses 24.3 kWh, so it costs $34 less a month. A Model Y Long Range AWD uses 27.5 kWh, so it costs $28 less a month.

Charging speed at home: a regular 120V outlet adds about 3 miles per hour. 32A Level 2 adds about 16; 40A Level 2 adds about 20; 48A Level 2 adds about 24 miles per hour, if the car's onboard charger accepts that much. How many hours a night that means.
