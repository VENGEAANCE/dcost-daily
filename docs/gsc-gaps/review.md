# Editorial decision for October 19 through November 24, 2026

**Publish zero new guides from this export.** The leading EV-cost questions have relevant destinations in the existing 96-page inventory. The two local charger searches are worth recording, but this export gives little evidence of demand and the available state rates do not establish local installation or public charging prices.

Google Search Console reported 936 impressions and five clicks for Web search over 37 days. The Queries table disclosed 24 queries, all with zero clicks. The Pages table contained 87 URLs. The export has no paired query and landing-page rows, so the pages below establish editorial coverage, not which URL Google displayed.

| Search intent | Evidence in disclosed queries | Reviewed destination and decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EV, gas savings and Tesla calculators | Nine searches containing “calculator” earned 11 impressions. The related “ev cost estimator” earned one more. | The [calculator](https://dcostdaily.com/calculator) already compares charging and gasoline costs. Keep one destination. |
| Georgia EV charging rates | Three impressions, average position 91.33 | The [Georgia page](https://dcostdaily.com/ev-cost/georgia) gives the residential price per kWh and model-level charging costs. The query does not specify public charging. |
| Tesla versus Toyota fuel cost | One impression, average position 1 | The [Model 3 vs. Camry comparison](https://dcostdaily.com/compare/tesla-model-3-vs-toyota-camry) and [hybrid guide](https://dcostdaily.com/guides/tesla-vs-hybrid-fuel-cost) already answer this question. |
| Electric car fuel cost | One impression, average position 92 | The [state cost index](https://dcostdaily.com/ev-cost/) and calculator cover this broad question. |
| Tesla charger cost in Denver or Fort Collins | One impression per city, average positions 84 and 73 respectively | Defer both. The wording could refer to hardware, installation or a public charging session. A city-specific answer needs evidence for the intended cost. |
| Brand, news and ambiguous searches | Includes “d'cost”, “djt ev power tech” and “price difference” | Exclude from the charger-guide backlog. |

The [automated candidate report](report.md) retains the query-level baselines and proposed URLs for audit. The decision above supersedes those proposals for this export. With zero new pages, there is no production release or post-release revenue measurement for this batch.
