Beyond the fuel billRange, home charging, tailpipe.

Range. The EPA rates the Tesla Cybertruck AWD at 325 miles on a charge. On a 48A home charger it adds about 24 miles an hour, so a typical day's driving (about 33 miles) goes back in 1.4 hr. Level 1 vs Level 2.

Tailpipe. The Silverado puts out 531 grams of CO2 per mile (EPA), about 531 kg over 1,000 miles. The Cybertruck has no tailpipe; its emissions depend on how your grid makes power.

Second opinion. fueleconomy.gov's own yearly fuel cost estimate, at 15,000 miles and its national prices, is $950 for the Cybertruck and $3,800 for the Silverado. See EPA's side by side.

What this leaves out. Purchase price, insurance and maintenance usually move the total more than fuel does, and Supercharging on road trips costs more than home power. The calculator has fields for payment, insurance and maintenance.
