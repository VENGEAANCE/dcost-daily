Gas would have to fall to $2.25 a gallon for the Camry Hybrid to tie, or home electricity climb to 34.4¢ per kWh.

Beyond the fuel billRange, home charging, tailpipe.

Range. The EPA rates the Tesla Model 3 Standard RWD at 321 miles on a charge. On a 48A home charger it adds about 43 miles an hour, so a typical day's driving (about 33 miles) goes back in 45 min. Level 1 vs Level 2.

Tailpipe. The Camry Hybrid puts out 173 grams of CO2 per mile (EPA), about 173 kg over 1,000 miles. The Model 3 has no tailpipe; its emissions depend on how your grid makes power.

Second opinion. fueleconomy.gov's own yearly fuel cost estimate, at 15,000 miles and its national prices, is $550 for the Model 3 and $1,250 for the Camry Hybrid. See EPA's side by side.

What this leaves out. Purchase price, insurance and maintenance usually move the total more than fuel does, and Supercharging on road trips costs more than home power. The calculator has fields for payment, insurance and maintenance.
