vs Honda Accord
$89 a month cheaper to fuel

$1,071 a year. That's the fuel saving for a 2026 Tesla Model 3 Standard RWD over a 2026 Honda Accord 1.5T at 12,000 miles a year on US average prices: $44 a month to charge at home vs $133 in gas. Electricity is 18.16¢ per kWh (August 2026, EIA) and regular gas is $4.27 a gallon (week of Oct 26, 2026, EIA). Gas would have to fall to $1.41 a gallon for the Accord to tie, or home electricity climb to 54.9¢ per kWh.

Beyond the fuel billRange, home charging, tailpipe.

Range. The EPA rates the Tesla Model 3 Standard RWD at 321 miles on a charge. On a 48A home charger it adds about 43 miles an hour, so a typical day's driving (about 33 miles) goes back in 45 min. Level 1 vs Level 2.

Tailpipe. The Accord puts out 276 grams of CO2 per mile (EPA), about 276 kg over 1,000 miles. The Model 3 has no tailpipe; its emissions depend on how your grid makes power.

Second opinion. fueleconomy.gov's own yearly fuel cost estimate, at 15,000 miles and its national prices, is $550 for the Model 3 and $2,050 for the Accord. See EPA's side by side.

What this leaves out. Purchase price, insurance and maintenance usually move the total more than fuel does, and Supercharging on road trips costs more than home power. The calculator has fields for payment, insurance and maintenance.
