That's the fuel saving for a 2026 Tesla Model Y Long Range AWD over a 2026 Toyota RAV4 Hybrid AWD (SE & LE) at 12,000 miles a year on US average prices: $50 a month to charge at home vs $102 in gas. Electricity is 18.16¢ per kWh (August 2026, EIA) and regular gas is $4.27 a gallon (week of Oct 26, 2026, EIA). Gas would have to fall to $2.10 a gallon for the RAV4 Hybrid to tie, or home electricity climb to 37.0¢ per kWh.

Beyond the fuel billRange, home charging, tailpipe.

Range. The EPA rates the Tesla Model Y Long Range AWD at 327 miles on a charge. On a 48A home charger it adds about 38 miles an hour, so a typical day's driving (about 33 miles) goes back in 50 min. Level 1 vs Level 2.

Tailpipe. The RAV4 Hybrid puts out 209 grams of CO2 per mile (EPA), about 209 kg over 1,000 miles. The Model Y has no tailpipe; its emissions depend on how your grid makes power.

Second opinion. fueleconomy.gov's own yearly fuel cost estimate, at 15,000 miles and its national prices, is $600 for the Model Y and $1,550 for the RAV4 Hybrid. See EPA's side by side.

What this leaves out. Purchase price, insurance and maintenance usually move the total more than fuel does, and Supercharging on road trips costs more than home power. The calculator has fields for payment, insurance and maintenance.
